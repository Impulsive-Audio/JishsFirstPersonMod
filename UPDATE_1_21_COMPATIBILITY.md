# 1.21 compatibility update for ReSkate 2.0.3

Adds a saved compatibility switch to the mod panel. Right-stick camera look and directional glances remain available, including sensitivity, bindings and look smoothing. The First Person checkbox, enter-first-person hotkey, Third person while bailing and First person on board only remain active.

In compatibility mode the mod passes through camera position, FOV, Spring and native stabilization, and disables head attachment, bail camera holds, foot/board transition smoothing and character turning. Existing saved settings are preserved for returning to full mode. Look input still pauses during bails, menus and unsupported player states; on-board right-stick input remains available for tricks.

Close Skate and extract `JishsFirstPersonMod-1.21-reskate-2.0.3-compatibility-update.zip` beside Skate.exe. It contains only Mods, scripts, dinput8.dll and LICENSE.txt. Use the standard launcher and open the mod panel with Insert.

Validation: all 24 Windows tests passed, including compatibility persistence, head-attachment bypass, manual activation forwarding and comfort-option edits. The final package was tested in-game and confirmed working.
