# Jish's First Person Mod 1.24

**Updated for ReSkate 2.0.6**

- Added **Toggle glance controls**: press a glance shortcut to hold that direction, press it again to return, or choose another direction to switch.
- **Bail improvements — WIP:** improved first-person head following, smoother stand-up camera movement and a smoother return to normal camera orientation. These changes are still being refined.
- Existing hold-to-glance controls remain available.

Close Skate and extract the ZIP into your ReSkate folder, replacing the existing mod files.
