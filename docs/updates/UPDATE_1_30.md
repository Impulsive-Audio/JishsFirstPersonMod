# 1.30 - Body-flip head lock and spectator view (development source)

Adds an optional Lock head during on-board body flips setting under Camera > Attachment and transitions. It defaults off, requires full mode and is independent of right-stick look, mounting and hippy-jump settings. Personal presets include it; existing files and the recommended preset keep it off.

The detector adapts ReSkate's body-up-axis tumble measurement. Verified on-board air states 200/201, increasing tilt above 30 degrees/second for 60 ms and body tilt of at least 80 degrees trigger the lock. Grabs are not required. Tilt below 60 degrees or a new airborne episode rearms detection; rotation below 20 degrees/second for 120 ms ends the lock.

Position and shortest-path quaternion rotation blend from the displayed view to the head pose with configured camera offsets over 250 ms, then follow directly. Landing, dismounting, disabling the option and settled rotation blend back. Bails and game-owned cameras take priority. Discontinuities reset stale history. Spring/stabilization and competing transition smoothing yield while head lock owns the view; calibration remains frozen during the handoff. Glances pause while trick inputs remain available.

Validation: all 35 Windows tests passed. Menu layouts were rendered and inspected at 100%, 150% and 200% DPI using the existing ReSkate fonts and colors. Automated detector, interpolation, calibration and persistence tests cover threshold behavior, flips/rolls, upright motion, settling, rearming, interrupted entry, discontinuities and moving targets. Gameplay validation is pending, especially slow flips, repeated flips, landing handoffs and near-vertical ramp transitions. No native flip-active or grab flag is used.

Diagnostics: bail-camera-trace-v11.csv adds body_flip_tilt, body_flip_rate, body_flip_phase (0 idle, 1 blending in, 2 locked, 3 blending out) and body_flip_head_lock. State changes also log tilt and rate.

Fast-flip handoffs now follow the moving destination while fading the initial local position/rotation difference over 250 ms. This prevents rapid head movement from creating extra separation from a fixed world-space blend anchor. Full lock remains direct; gameplay validation of the revised handoff is pending.

Front-flip offset fix: the flip target uses animated head pose plus configured position/rotation offsets directly. It no longer inherits the transient native-camera/head delta used by bail calibration. Regression tests cover both pitch directions, zero offsets, configured offsets and a Spring-lagged normal camera. Gameplay validation of this fix is pending.

First-person spectator view is available under Camera > Attachment and transitions, default off. While ReSkate 2.0.7 follows a party player in a throwdown, Game of Skate or manual spectating, the existing First Person hotkey/controller binding switches between the follow camera and the watched head view. Each session begins in follow view; player changes retain the selected view, and the local First Person preference remains unchanged. The view uses ReSkate's eye midpoint, face clearance and camera axes, with configured offsets/FOV and the current Spring/stabilization settings. A same-frame render update keeps it aligned with animated geometry. View changes retain 250 ms handoffs and local render-only head hiding. Invalid or stale targets retain the follow camera. ReSkate keeps camera ownership and turn restoration. Remote look input and camera settings are not transmitted. See [spectator verification](../testing/TEST_SPECTATOR.md).
