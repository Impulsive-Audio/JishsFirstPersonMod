# Jish's First Person Mod 1.27 — ReSkate 2.0.7

Updated for ReSkate 2.0.7.

- **Third person while getting up:** Keep falls in first person, then switch to third person while getting up. First person returns when you regain control. Turn off “Third person while bailing” to use this option.
- **Bald in first person:** Keeps long hair out of your view. Your hairstyle returns in third person, and other players still see your original hair.
- **Fewer accidental emotes:** Improved blocking when using LB/L1 + D-pad to enter or exit first person, including quick repeated presses.
- **Bail camera collision — WIP:** Added collision with floors and objects, plus faster head following and camera recovery. The camera can still stick near surfaces; enable “Third person while getting up” as an alternative while this is being improved.
- Updated menu help to explain the new options.

Close Skate and extract Mods, scripts, dinput8.dll and LICENSE.txt into your ReSkate folder. Your saved settings are kept.
